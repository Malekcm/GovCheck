# Comprehensive federal BD discovery and capture question bank

**Output for every question:** ID; current best answer; status; evidence URL/file; checked-on date; owner; recommended evidence request; urgency. Questions that cannot be answered remain explicit OPEN records. Adapt by opportunity but do not omit hard gates.

## A. Acquisition identity and access
- A01: What is the agency, subagency, buying office, end user and mission owner?
- A02: What is the exact forecast ID, SAM notice ID, RFI, RFQ, RFP and amendment trail?
- A03: Is it planning, sources sought, RFI, draft RFP, active competition, closed, canceled, award, or justification?
- A04: Is there an actual current solicitation with government-hosted PWS/SOW/evaluation factors?
- A05: Is it public on SAM.gov, gated on eBuy, other portal or invitation-only? Can our authorized employee access it?
- A06: What are the first issue, revised issue, questions, response, oral, award and POP dates in local timezone?
- A07: Is it a recompete, consolidation, bridge, replacement, continuation, new requirement or change in buying office?
- A08: Are there forecast version changes and conflicting notices requiring reconciliation?
- A09: Are sources sought or industry day slides, Q&A, one-on-ones, and prior RFIs available?
- A10: Have any amendments changed eligibility, mandatory sections or deadlines?

## B. Vehicle, acquisition and eligibility
- B01: Which schedule, multiple award contract, IDIQ, GWAC, BPA or commercial route is required?
- B02: Is it OASIS+ SB, unrestricted, another MAC, and what exact domain/CLIN/NAICS?
- B03: Is our specific *legal entity* active on that contract vehicle and authorized in that exact ordering lane?
- B04: What is the size standard in current NAICS, based on receipts or employees, and our entity/affiliates position?
- B05: What set-aside (SB, 8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, HUBZone) applies now versus forecast?
- B06: Can we prime, team or subcontract, and which bidders or similarly situated partners qualify?
- B07: What limitations on subcontracting and ostensible subcontractor / unusual reliance risks exist?
- B08: Are JV requirements, contractor team agreements, mentor-protege or CO approvals relevant?
- B09: What performance location, citizenship, facility and onsite staffing requirements apply?
- B10: What clearance, background investigation, PIV/CAC, ATO, data classification and security controls apply?
- B11: Are there OCI, impaired objectivity, government employee conflict, nondisclosure and incumbent data restrictions?
- B12: Are there special licenses, professional registrations, certifications or mandatory insurance/bonding?
- B13: Is contract type FFP, T&M, labor-hour, cost reimbursable or hybrid and where is ceiling/funding risk allocated?
- B14: Is award single or multiple? What is the estimated number of awards and task-order competition method?
- B15: Does acquisition history show protests, corrective action, changes in contract vehicle or canceled attempts?

## C. Contract value and economics
- C01: What is the forecast band, estimated total, IDIQ ceiling, maximum awarded value and actual obligated value?
- C02: How much was funded per year on the predecessor and on recent order calls? What is recurring vs surge?
- C03: What base period, options, ordering period, transition window and extensions are anticipated?
- C04: What is the Government's IGCE or verified comparable historical labor/FTE demand?
- C05: What labor categories, wage determinations, indirect/ODC/travel and place-of-performance costs apply?
- C06: Are rates, escalation assumptions, salary compliance and local-market recruiting feasible?
- C07: What milestone, acceptance, SLAs, KPIs and invoice terms drive FFP financial and performance risk?
- C08: What is downside margin and cash impact at staffing vacancy/surge/overrun sensitivity cases?
- C09: What is realistic capture/proposal investment and cost of opportunity if pursuit fails?
- C10: Are subcontractor flow-down, late payments, retention and performance guarantees acceptable?

## D. Customer and mission
- D01: Why is agency buying now? Mission restructuring, legislation, technology debt, oversight finding, contract end?
- D02: Which executives/program managers actually own results and budget?
- D03: What are mission outcomes, priorities, pain points, transformation deadlines and current failure modes?
- D04: What incumbent services continue uninterrupted versus net-new capabilities?
- D05: How do PWS workstreams map to customer organizations, functions and desired outcomes?
- D06: Which official agency strategy, budget, IT plan, GAO/IG reports or industry-day statements define priorities?
- D07: Which key stakeholders, procurement POCs, small business specialists and technical influencers can be lawfully contacted?
- D08: Have we engaged program office through authorized channels and recorded verified needs?
- D09: What contractor deliverables, governance cadence, metrics and decision rights are required?
- D10: Are there reporting/data ownership/technology stack dependencies that make transition difficult?

## E. Incumbency and competition
- E01: Who is current prime? What is the exact incumbent legal entity/UEI, prime vehicle and parent contract?
- E02: What was the predecessor RFQ/RFP, effective PWS, award date and contracting strategy?
- E03: What is incumbent parent vehicle ceiling, funded obligations, spend trend, POP and task orders?
- E04: Which delivery offices, labor roles, subs and partner firms are visible in legitimate public sources?
- E05: Are there open incumbent bridge orders, modifications, new related awards or exercised options?
- E06: What prior solicitation evaluation factors and agency mission needs could carry over, with historical caveat?
- E07: What experience, clearances, incumbent retained personnel and stakeholder relationships create transition barriers?
- E08: Does current set-aside/vehicle constrain incumbent eligibility? Could a JV or affiliate be eligible?
- E09: Who else holds the exact needed vehicle domain and has customer-relevant past performance?
- E10: What differentiators could be evidenced rather than merely claimed?
- E11: What direct evidence exists for incumbent performance? Do not claim deficiencies without reliable sourcing.
- E12: Are there protests/GAO decisions, sole-source justifications or oversight findings materially affecting competition?

## F. Scope, technical and delivery fit
- F01: What is each PWS requirement, deliverable and acceptance clause? Exact current RFP citation?
- F02: Which requirements are core PMO, portfolio/schedule/risk, budget, cost and decision support?
- F03: Which are data ingestion, analytics, reporting, integration, APIs, governance and cybersecurity?
- F04: Which are enterprise/solution/data architecture, technology strategy, standards and system modernization?
- F05: Which are systems engineering lifecycle, SELC, acquisition and specialized mission engineering?
- F06: Which are asset logistics, lifecycle maintenance, facilities/real property and owner representation?
- F07: Which are strategic communications, adoption, workforce change or organization transformation?
- F08: Does the company have approved prime or sub past performance *for each workstream*?
- F09: What contract IDs, value, actual role/workshare, dates, CPARS, accepted artifacts and customer approvals prove fit?
- F10: Are user/company identified STRONG abilities validated by formal project artifacts? What is missing?
- F11: What are the biggest capability gaps and which are make, hire, partner or walk-away?
- F12: Which named PM, chief engineer, architect, data lead, controls lead, logistics lead etc. are actually available?
- F13: What clearances/citizenship, credentials, geography and contingency staffing are needed?
- F14: Can we execute transition-in, preserve operations, obtain government access and own delivery accountability?
- F15: Do we have validated labor mapping, compliant workshare, subcontract oversight and an emergency staffing bench?
- F16: Can we offer measurable technical win themes linked to buyer priorities and differentiating evidence?

## G. Proposal and evaluation
- G01: What are all solicitation sections L/M (or equivalent), volumes, instructions, evaluation factors and weights?
- G02: Are mandatory pass/fail certifications, past-performance recency/relevance or registrations required?
- G03: Does evaluation use lowest-price, best-value tradeoff, technical/risk/adjectival scoring, oral or demonstration?
- G04: What are question deadlines, instructions, page limits, editable forms, naming, Q&A and submission system?
- G05: Is a facility clearance, DCAA financial system or mandatory subcontracting plan required?
- G06: What nonstandard indemnities, data rights, property, OCI or labor terms need legal review?
- G07: Do prior or current notices reveal the government evaluation philosophy, without assuming old criteria still apply?
- G08: What is the proposal compliance crosswalk including each clause, page limit and attachment?
- G09: What is pricing basis, staffing volume, labor mix and required evidence behind each price CLIN?
- G10: Do capture and delivery leadership approve technical solution, staffing, pricing, commercial risk, and final bid?
- G11: What is the partner LOI / exclusivity / resume consent / reference authorization plan?
- G12: What are the hard no-bid triggers and the latest defensible decision date?

## H. Decision gates and operational follow-up
- H01: Are current RFP existence/access, vehicle/domain, legal eligibility, security/OCI, staffing, workshare and economic feasibility resolved?
- H02: Which information remains OPEN and what exact evidence would close it?
- H03: What is current G0/G1/G2/G3/G4/G5 gate and who can approve advancement?
- H04: What is the weighted opportunity score, with unknown dimensions N/A rather than invented?
- H05: What is the decision log, risk owner, milestone and due date for every blocker?
- H06: Who will retrieve restricted eBuy, customer call notes, approved corporate references and resumes?
- H07: Which official forecast changes, awards, notices and amendments should be rechecked on future refresh?
- H08: After award, rejection or cancelation, which outcome and lessons should be stored for reuse?

## Special trigger: no solicitation found
Do not stop research. Deliver predecessor PWS, official forecast, contract/domain evidence, other notices, incumbent awards, capture contacts and questions to the CO. Put **CURRENT SOLICITATION NOT VERIFIED** at top with eBuy/SAM paths and an assigned action. All proposal compliance fields remain OPEN, not fabricated.
